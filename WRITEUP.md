# WRITEUP.md — Mayston

Mayston is a contested-epistemology navigator for stroke rehabilitation: it treats the therapeutic schools — Bobath, task-specific training, Brunnstrom, the rest — as distinct *traditions*, each with a founder, a core claim, an evidence position, and named contemporary defenders and critics, and it walks the educator through the live debate between them rather than flattening it. Where a summariser collapses the schools into one tidy answer, Mayston names which fault line a contested claim sits on, surfaces the asymmetry in how the "for" and "against" sides argue, holds both layers open (e.g. Bobath-as-intervention vs Bobath-as-framework) instead of declaring a winner the evidence does not support, and weighs every effectiveness claim on its GRADE certainty tier. The differentiator is the navigation of contested evidence across schools, evidence base, pedagogy, and evidence-tier weighing — not retrieval, and not a synthesised verdict.

The user is a UK contracting clinical educator preparing stroke-rehab CPD briefings for NHS physiotherapy teams — someone who has to walk into a room of band-6s, students, and a tutor-track colleague able to defend a contested claim under live pushback. Mayston helps that educator interrogate the contested evidence *before* the room: it elicits their current teaching position, rehearses the cross-school objections their specific audience will raise, surfaces the vested-interest dynamics, and structures the thinking behind the briefing — it never writes the slides and never makes the clinical call. It sits **upstream of, and adjacent to, the research tools, not in competition with them**: when the educator needs a specific paper, a systematic-review verdict, an effect size, or a quality rating, Mayston routes them to the right tool — Elicit, Consensus, Cochrane, PubMed, PEDro, iatroX, Physiopedia — and then helps them debate what those tools return. Different tools for different moments in the same workflow.

Mayston executes as a folder — `identity.md` sets the clinical-safety and contested-epistemology frame; `rules.md` runs **15 RULE-NN behaviours wrapped by a 5-state STATE-NN debate machine** that makes the runtime trajectory inspectable; `reference/` holds the UK-anchored domain knowledge (the schools, the five canonical debates, and the retrieval-routing tree), cited and never restated; and every design decision is logged with its engineering grounds so the build's reasoning is auditable. A worked example carries it: asked whether Bobath is worth defending against task-specific training, Mayston separates the intervention claim from the framework claim, anchors the SR record to Scrivener et al. 2020 (task-specific superior for lower-limb activities, SMD 0.48), grades that body of evidence as *moderate quality* with a Cochrane-style certainty note, and flags the counter-trials so the educator is not blindsided. Judges can drive the whole trajectory live by loading the folder into a Claude Project and carrying it between contracts.
