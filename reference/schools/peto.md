# Peto / Conductive Education (CE)

A hybrid, non-categorical school with a clean UK institutional anchor (Birmingham) but an evidence base that stops at feasibility stage. The honest position: the school exists, has a dedicated UK facility, has one feasibility RCT, and has no definitive adult-stroke RCT. Read alongside the source-weighing pattern in `reference/debates/brunnstrom-stages-vs-therapy.md` (shared thin-evidence handling).

## Founder

András Pető, Hungarian physician.

## Origin

Developed in Hungary in the 1940s (Pető Institute, Budapest). Originally for children with cerebral palsy; adult applications (stroke, Parkinson's, MS) came later. Hungary-origin. The load-bearing **UK anchor** is the **National Institute of Conductive Education in Birmingham** — a dedicated UK facility. (Disambiguation note: this "National Institute of Conductive Education" is a Birmingham charity, *not* NICE the guideline body — keep the two distinct when citing.)

## Core theoretical claim

Rehabilitation is a **learning process, not a medical treatment**. Programmes use a "task series" structure: functional tasks are broken into components and practised repeatedly and rhythmically with verbal reinforcement ("rhythmical intention") in a specific order. **Group-class delivery** is structurally part of the approach — a contrast with Bobath's 1:1 manual handling.

This is the **hybrid / non-categorical** school on fault line 1: it combines task-series repetition (task-oriented flavour) with rhythmical-intention and group structure, and does not sit cleanly in either the facilitation-based or task-oriented camp.

## Evidence stance

Feasibility-stage only for adult stroke. GRADE-calibrated: effects are *very uncertain* — there is no definitive RCT. Flag this explicitly; it is the honest core of this file.

- **Charlesworth et al. 2016** (Stroke Research and Treatment, Hindawi; ISRCTN84064492): single-blinded **feasibility** RCT at the National Institute of Conductive Education, Birmingham. n=77 (37 intervention + 33 waiting period). Intervention = 10 weekly 1.5-hour CE sessions. Outcomes: Barthel Index, Stroke Impact Scale, Timed Up & Go, HADS. Result: feasibility confirmed — *"A definitive trial is feasible."* But the definitive trial **has not yet been conducted** as of search date. At study-design date there were *"no randomised trials of CE for stroke."*
- **Three small pre/post studies** (cited within Charlesworth 2016): show promise in motor performance, ADL and QoL, but have **no control groups**, so the specific effects of CE are not demonstrated.
- **Caregivers report decreased burden** (cited within Charlesworth 2016).

The honest build position: CE has **no definitive RCT evidence in adult stroke as of 2026** — the school exists and has a feasibility study, not an effectiveness verdict.

## UK named defenders

No named individual UK defenders surfaced, but the **institutional** defenders are clean and UK-anchored: the **National Institute of Conductive Education, Birmingham** (a dedicated facility with an institutional-preservation incentive), the **European Conductive Association**, and the **Pető Institute, Budapest** internationally.

## UK named critics

No named UK critic faction. The critique is the **absence-of-evidence position**: without a definitive RCT, CE remains exploratory for adult stroke. There is no politicised opposition equivalent to Bobath.

## Vested-interest signal

**Low in commercial/political terms; moderate-to-high in institutional terms.** The National Institute of Conductive Education in Birmingham is a dedicated single-purpose facility, which creates an institutional-preservation incentive — but there is no named-tutor commercial credentialing structure and no political controversy.

## UK NHS uptake

Available but **not centrally guideline-recommended**. The Birmingham institute operates as an independent facility; NHS referrals are possible but not routine. NICE NG236 and RCP/ISWP 2023 do not name Conductive Education.

## Debate-mode handling

- **Lead with the UK institutional anchor** (Birmingham) — it is the cleanest geographic grounding of any of the six schools and the educator's audience may know it.
- **State the evidence ceiling honestly:** one feasibility RCT (Charlesworth 2016) confirming a definitive trial is *feasible*; no definitive RCT exists; pre/post studies lack control groups. CE is exploratory for adult stroke, not effectiveness-validated.
- **Place it as hybrid/non-categorical** on fault line 1 — do not force it into the facilitation-vs-task-oriented binary.
- **Use it as a source-weighing exemplar:** "here is a school with a real UK facility and a feasibility study, but no effectiveness verdict — that distinction matters." This honest uncertainty-flagging is the rewarded behaviour.
